# Finding ideas with Semrush

How to find site and page ideas that get traffic a new site can actually win.
This is the process used to pick the Turkish tools site and the tier 1 quiz
ideas. Write what you find into [../IDEA.md](../IDEA.md).

## What makes a good idea

| Signal | Good | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keyword Difficulty (KD) | 0 to 29 (easy), 30 to 49 (possible) | A new site with few links can reach page 1 |
| Volume | Many keywords adding up, not one big head term | The long tail is easier and steadier |
| Intent | The searcher wants to *do* something (calculate, test, play, convert) | AI answers cannot replace a tool or a game |
| SERP | Top 10 has small sites, weak pages, forums, news articles | Proof that strong sites are not there |
| Competitors | Young sites with low Authority Score growing fast | Proof that a new site can win here |
| Ads | Broad, safe audience; repeat visits | Ad networks pay on volume and session length |

## Step 1: Brainstorm seed keywords

List 50 to 150 short "head" terms for the kind of site you want: tools
(timer, percentage calculator), games (sudoku, flag quiz), tests (typing
test, color blind test), learning (times tables), in **every language you
might build in**. Write each in the local language, not translated word for
word.

## Step 2: Bulk check (Keyword Overview > Bulk Analysis)

1. Open **Keyword Overview**, switch to **Bulk Analysis**.
2. Paste up to **100 keywords**, one per line.
3. Pick the **country database** (each country separately: US, UK, DE, TR...).
4. Export or copy volume, KD, intent and CPC.

Group the results into ideas and add up the volume, and the volume at KD 29
or below, per idea and per country. English head terms are usually KD 60 to
95; the same tools in Turkish, Polish, Indonesian or Arabic are often 20 to 35.

## Step 3: Size the long tail (Keyword Magic Tool)

For each promising idea:

1. Open **Keyword Magic Tool**, enter the seed ("hesaplama", "kaç gün kaldı",
   "trivia"), choose the country.
2. Read the totals at the top: number of keywords, total volume, average KD.
   A seed with 100,000+ keywords and average KD under 30 is a big, soft market.
3. Use **Groups** on the left to see sub-topics.
4. Filter **KD to 0 to 29** and sort by volume: these are your first pages.
5. Use **Questions** for FAQ ideas.
6. Remove noise: brand names, people's names, unrelated meanings, and old
   year variants ("2022", "2023") that inflate volume.
7. Look at **trend** for seasonality (exam calculators peak before results,
   holiday countdowns before the holiday).

## Step 4: Check the SERP (Keyword Overview > SERP analysis)

For the 20 to 40 most important keywords, open the keyword and look at the
top 10 results:

- **Page Authority Score** and **referring domains** of each ranking URL.
  Pages with AS under 20 and fewer than 20 referring domains are beatable.
- **Who** ranks: a dedicated tool site, or news, forums, Wikipedia, an
  airline page? The second kind means nobody owns the topic yet.
- **SERP features**: Google's own calculator, timer or answer box takes
  clicks. Treat those keywords as a base, not a growth engine.

## Step 5: Study competitors (Domain Overview, Organic Research)

For the 10 to 20 domains that keep appearing:

1. **Domain Overview**: organic traffic, number of keywords, Authority
   Score, referring domains, and the traffic trend over 12 months.
2. Look for **young sites that grew fast** with low Authority Score: they
   prove the market is open. A big old leader that is shrinking is a gap.
3. **Organic Research > Positions**: their top pages and keywords show which
   pages to build first.
4. **Keyword Gap**: enter 3 to 5 competitors to find keywords they rank for
   that the others miss.

## Step 6: Decide and plan

Write in IDEA.md:

- total volume and volume at KD 29 or below, per country;
- the 3 to 5 competitors and what they prove;
- the launch page list: page, main keyword, volume, KD;
- seasonality and the launch date it implies;
- risks (formulas that change, Google answer boxes, low ad rates).

Go if the easy volume is in the millions per month and small sites are
already winning. Pass if the top 10 is all big brands with AS 50+.

## Limits to know

- Semrush accounts have a **daily report limit**. Bulk Analysis (100 per
  run) is the cheapest way to check many keywords; Keyword Magic Tool and
  SERP checks use more of the limit. Plan the big runs.
- Volumes are estimates; treat them as relative, not exact.
- KD is per country. Always pick the right database.
